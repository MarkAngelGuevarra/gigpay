# GigPay Pilot — Privacy Notice

_Last updated: November 5, 2026_

GigPay is a pilot running on **Stellar Testnet** as part of the Stellar Philippines Instawards program. Testnet XLM is practice money with no cash value. This notice explains what the pilot collects and why, in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

## What we collect
- **Account:** your email address and the role you choose (client or freelancer). Your password is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and is never visible to us.
- **Pilot activity:** the tasks you create or accept, proof-of-work links you submit, and dispute notes.
- **Wallet:** your Stellar Testnet wallet address. A practice wallet is created in your browser; its key stays in that browser.
- **Feedback:** anything you choose to tell us in the tester form or by email.

## Why we collect it
- To run the pilot: show your tasks, sync them across devices, and let the escrow pay the right address.
- To improve GigPay from your feedback.
- To report pilot results to the Instawards program. We report **totals** (for example, number of wallets and completed escrows). We do not publish your email.

## What is public
Transactions on **Stellar Testnet are public**: anyone can see a wallet address, the escrows it funded or received, and their amounts on explorers such as Stellar Expert. GigPay's public evidence table lists wallet addresses and transaction links, never emails or names.

## Who can see your data
The GigPay team, and the service providers that host the pilot (Supabase for accounts and tasks, Vercel for the website, Google Forms for feedback). We do not sell your data or share your email with anyone else.

## How long we keep it
For the duration of the pilot and the Instawards program reporting. You can ask us to delete your account and pilot data at any time. Transactions already on Stellar Testnet cannot be removed by anyone, but we will stop linking them to you.

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, by emailing **Mark Angel Guevarra, GigPay** at [marcangelguevarra@gmail.com](mailto:marcangelguevarra@gmail.com).
